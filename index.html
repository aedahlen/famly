Why Famly

From both sides of the tiny chair

I spent my own early years sitting in a tiny chair in California. And now I have a nearly 3 year old daughter who sits in a tiny Artek chair at home in Copenhagen. What draws me to Famly is the opportunity to design a product where better digital experiences can create more time and space for human connection.

As a parent myself, I understand how much communication, coordination and admin surrounds childcare and how valuable it is when technology quietly makes that easier. I believe in Famly's values of bringing joy, play, connection and kindness to this world.

So there is time for what matters.
How I work now

AI changed what I consider my job to be

I started designing when Sketch was still the main tool, and much of the craft was in producing the design itself. That's shifting further towards design direction: understanding the problem, finding the solutions, then using AI to explore and build faster. It creates more room to test, learn and play instead of getting attached to the first idea. It also frees up my time to focus on what matters: human experience.

Faster production makes design judgement matter more, not less. I still need to understand the behaviour behind an interaction, keep it consistent with the design system, consider accessibility, and decide what feels clear, useful and human. This mockup, for one, went from concept to clickable with Claude rather than starting in Figma.
